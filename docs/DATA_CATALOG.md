# SupplyPulse data catalog

_Generated by `python -m supplypulse.catalog.build_catalog`; do not edit by hand._

## Source inventory

- **DataCo Smart Supply Chain** (Constante, Silva & Pereira (2019), Mendeley Data), license CC BY 4.0
- Format: CSV, ISO-8859-1, one row per order item; coverage: 2015-01-01 to 2018-01-31 order dates
- Rows: raw 180,519, curated 180,519, quarantined 0; columns kept 42 of 53
- Dropped as PII or empty: Customer Email, Customer Fname, Customer Lname, Customer Password, Customer Street, Customer Zipcode, Order Zipcode, Order Customer Id, Order Item Cardprod Id, Product Description, Product Image

Known limitations:

- No inventory levels, supplier ids or shipped-vs-ordered quantities: OTIF and inventory turns cannot be computed.
- Product Status is constant, so a stock-availability KPI was dropped.
- Shipping outcomes are almost deterministic by shipping mode (for example First Class is late on every line).
- Weekly order volume is nearly constant until Oct 2017, then drops about 60% with a narrower product range.

## Governed metrics (11)

| Metric | Definition | Unit | Better when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Order lines** (`order_lines`) | Count of order items. | count | up |
| **Orders** (`orders`) | Distinct orders. | count | up |
| **Units sold** (`units_sold`) | Sum of item quantity. | units | up |
| **Net sales** (`net_sales`) | Gross sales minus item discount. | usd | up |
| **Profit** (`profit`) | Sum of order profit per item. | usd | up |
| **Profit margin** (`profit_margin`) | Profit divided by net sales. | ratio | up |
| **On-time delivery rate** (`on_time_delivery_rate`) | Lines delivered on time or early divided by lines not shipping-cancelled. This is OTD, not OTIF, because the data has no shipped-quantity field. | ratio | up |
| **Late delivery rate** (`late_delivery_rate`) | Late lines divided by lines not shipping-cancelled. | ratio | down |
| **Average delay (days)** (`avg_delay_days`) | Mean of actual minus scheduled shipping days over lines not shipping-cancelled. Negative means early. | days | down |
| **Order cancellation rate** (`cancellation_rate`) | Lines with order status CANCELED divided by all lines. | ratio | down |
| **Suspected fraud rate** (`suspected_fraud_rate`) | Lines with order status SUSPECTED_FRAUD divided by all lines. | ratio | down |
